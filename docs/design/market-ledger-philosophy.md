# Market Ledger

Market Ledger treats information as a field of measured marks: disciplined rows, restrained rules, and deliberate intervals form a visual system that can be understood before it is read. Space is never decorative. Every gap establishes hierarchy, every alignment reinforces comparison, and every repeated unit reflects painstaking, master-level calibration.

Color behaves as a signal rather than atmosphere. A near-black structural frame gives the work authority; warm yellow identifies the system; red and green appear only where direction must be understood immediately. The palette is meticulously controlled so that dense information remains calm, legible, and useful.

Scale creates rhythm. A decisive title and a small group of aggregate figures establish the whole, while compact repeated rows reveal the parts. The composition should feel like the work of an expert editor who has refined every baseline and column through countless iterations, balancing density against breathing room without wasting a pixel.

Typography is sparse, precise, and functional. Numeric forms align like instruments; names remain humane but compact; labels recede until needed. Text is not prose laid over design. It is a visual material integrated into the grid, crafted with the same painstaking attention as line weight, spacing, and color.

The final object should feel permanent despite describing one day. It is an archival market artifact: clinical but not cold, systematic but not generic, and executed with the quiet confidence of someone at the top of the field. Its craft is visible in consistency, not ornament.
